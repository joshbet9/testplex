# Modern Arcade mechanics prototypes (Paper 26.3)

This plugin adds first-pass playable mechanics harnesses for the 17 Arcade games outside the dedicated Monster Maze and Snake modules. It is a compatibility/work-in-progress layer intended to expose API and game-loop problems early; it is **not** a claim that all original Mineplex mechanics or maps have been faithfully ported yet.

## Current controlled acceptance queue

The shared Paper 26.3 Testplex allow-list contains 20 games:

Death Tag, One in the Quiver, Bomb Lobbers, Bacon Brawl, Gladiators, Micro Battles, Paintball, Plexball, Spleef, Super Sploor, Dragon Escape, Dragons, Runner, Sheep Quest, Turf Wars, Snap (legacy ID `camera-shy`), Monster Maze, Snake, Draw My Thing and Master Builders.

`READY_FOR_TEST` means available for the isolated shared test session. It does not mean the game has passed live multiplayer or map-by-map acceptance. See the game-specific acceptance plans for the required checks. Evolution, Castle Assault, Heroes of Gwen, Milk the Cow, Wither Assault, SkyWars Mixed and Sneaky Assassins remain gated.

## SkyWars — fast Mixed Arcade FFA

SkyWars is registered as the isolated `skywars-mixed` variant for Paper 26.3. It uses the archived 1.8 SkyWars maps and authored player-island spawns, and accepts up to 16 players without modifying the legacy Solo SkyWars or Team SkyWars classes.

The fast format uses a 5-second preparation window, an 8-minute live-round ceiling, a block-by-block distance-priority island crumble beginning 60 seconds after the fight starts, and a 45-second world-border close to 32 blocks. The shared generator holds two throwable TNT until collected, then recharges 15 seconds later. Throwable TNT keeps the source 60-tick fuse, 1.6 throw strength and 500 ms click recharge; left-click throws, right-click drops. Island, connector and middle chest pools use the original item families, weights, spawn probabilities and marker groups. The original RED-point Zombie Guardians are spawned with their 15-health gold-armour setup, tether, fire resistance and middle-loot death drop.

The lobby kit selector exposes the five legacy SkyWars kits: Ice (temporary bridge), Fire (area burst), Air (Void Saver and double jump), Metal (armour scaling and Magnet) and Earth (recharging Throwable Dirt). A nearest-target compass is provided to alive players and remains usable by eliminated spectators.

Join with `/arcadeport join skywars-mixed`, quick-start with `/arcadeport play skywars-mixed`, or start the queued lobby with `/arcadeport start skywars-mixed`. Choose an installed map with `/arcadeport maps skywars`.

SkyWars remains a manual-acceptance candidate, not a claim of complete 1.8 parity. The port still needs local Maven compilation and in-game validation, including all archived maps, loot rolls, each kit under combat, guardian pathing/death rewards, TNT damage and disconnect cleanup.

## Camera Shy — spyglass photographic elimination

Camera Shy is an in-process free-for-all: hold right-click with the vanilla spyglass to aim and press F (swap hands) to take a photograph. Five successful photographs eliminate a player. The camera has server-side line-of-sight and range validation, a shutter cooldown and short exposure protection; taking a photograph reveals the photographer's approximate position. The three-minute round ends with the last human player standing, while eliminated players spectate.

The lobby offers Smoke Artist (smoke screen), Decoy (fake snapshot), Runner (short Speed II escape burst), Spotter (nearby-player scan) and Understudy (player-skin stand-in). A rare Group Photo pickup can photograph two opponents in one frame; Director's Spotlight periodically highlights a player; Final Snapshot speeds up the shutter in the last 30 seconds. The dedicated maze is isolated from the lobby and other game worlds. Original human-player state is checkpointed to the plugin config before match setup, restored on a normal finish or reconnect, and retained across an unclean server restart.

`/stats camera-shy` exposes Camera Shy's lifetime record, milestone progress and all 20 achievements across four pages. The three lifetime milestones are 250 wins, 500 eliminations and 1,000 photographs. Camera Shy is now in the shared controlled READY_FOR_TEST queue; live multiplayer acceptance remains outstanding.

## Super Sploor — five-kit territory shooter

Super Sploor is a team-based territory shooter inspired by NANO's Sploor. The Paper 26.3 port uses its original archived Sploor map, an authoritative paint/territory engine, and five distinct kits: Sprayer, Roller, Charger, Lobber and Dualies. It is available in the shared controlled READY_FOR_TEST queue; live team-balance, map-spawn, ink/territory, squid-movement, special-ability and match-cleanup acceptance remains outstanding. See `../docs/SUPER-SPLOOR-DESIGN.md` for the mechanics baseline.

## Wizards — shared Arcade lifecycle prototype

Wizards is now hosted by `ArcadeMechanicsModern` rather than requiring its own runtime plugin to own the match. Its existing 21 spell effects, four source kits, loot/chest progression, mana/wand loop, endgame and temporary spell cleanup remain in the Wizards controller. Mage receives the source two-random-spell live-start bonus using each spell's original loot weight; Sorcerer, Mystic and Witch Doctor retain only Mana Bolt and Wizard's Compass as starter spells.

Use the shared Arcade entry points:

- `/arcadeport play wizards` — join and trigger a short quick-start window.
- `/arcadeport join wizards` — join the normal shared queue.
- `/arcadeport start wizards` — operator-only force start.
- `/arcadeport leave wizards` — leave the queue or active match.
- `/arcadeport kit wizards <mage|sorcerer|mystic|witchdoctor>` — select a kit while queued.
- `/arcadeport status` and `/arcadeport stop` — inspect or stop the shared test session.

Wizards takes exclusive ownership of the Arcade test session: it stops an existing Arcade match before taking player snapshots, prevents another game from starting while its queue/match is active, and restores players to their pre-match shared-lobby state on elimination, leave, completion, disconnect/reconnect or server shutdown. It writes lifetime wins, kills and spell-binding counts to the common local stats configuration.

This remains a mechanics-in-progress test port, not a READY_FOR_TEST game. Wizards discovers both the original Arcade `Wizards` map directory and the `Nano Wizards` compatibility folder through the shared Paper 26.3 source-map importer. The legacy Nano `GameType.WIZARDS` explicitly selects `mapsFromArcade(GameDisplay.Wizards)`, so source map WorldConfig spawn/chest markers drive the match arena. Map ZIPs can be bundled in the plugin or discovered from the server-local directory configured by `source-maps.directory` (default `update/maps`, relative to the server process working directory). An absolute path can be configured when the existing Reborn map store is elsewhere. External archives are constrained to the approved Wizards folders and rejected when paths traverse outside the map root or include symbolic links. The generated arena remains a fallback only when neither source folder contains an archive. The Git tree contains the `Nano Wizards/uses_arcade.txt` compatibility marker but does not mirror the original map ZIPs. The ten-map catalogue, builder credits and exact standard/Nano download URLs are recorded in `docs/wizards-source-map-catalogue.md`. A dedicated `Validate Wizards Source Maps` GitHub Actions workflow downloads all 20 variants into temporary runner storage, checks ZIP integrity and the exact world-root/WorldConfig/terrain structure required by this importer, records spawn and map-marker metadata plus SHA-256 hashes, then deletes the binary downloads; it uploads only the JSON/Markdown report. Live Paper 26.3 world loading and multi-client/reconnect acceptance remain outstanding. Mage's two source-weighted random starter spells, original 0.5 melee-damage cap, source armour-per-piece reductions and Wizards sidebar are preserved. Wizards is deliberately not added to the normal playable-game allow-list.

## Acceptance boundary

Install PacketEvents 2.14.0 on the Paper 26.3 test server for both the optional Bacon Brawl packet-hit path and Dragon Escape's genuine translucent player ghost. PacketEvents is a provided/soft dependency and is not bundled into `ArcadeMechanicsModern.jar`; the plugin logs a clear warning and cannot render the player ghost without it.

CI verifies compilation, plugin metadata and catalogue/ID tests. It does not verify the mechanics in a running server. The in-game iteration should exercise all enabled games in the same test sessions, including command/GUI entry, map loading, start/end, combat interactions, elimination/respawn, per-world cleanup and inventory restoration. Record game-specific defects and fix them without requiring testers to complete one game before trying another.

Game-specific cosmetic renderers are still deferred for most games, but Paintball weapon/pellet selections, Spleef block-break signatures and the Dragon Escape leader marker are now wired to their live gameplay hooks. Distinct win effects remain deferred until each game's mechanics and round lifecycle are stable.


## Dragon Escape race-timing acceptance

Dragon Escape has a source-comparison checklist for the PB ghost and live race timing:

1. Complete a full map once so the test server saves a map-specific PB and its checkpoint splits. Use `/stats` → Arcade → Dragon Escape to inspect the map PB list.
2. Toggle the personal ghost with `/arcadeport ghost on` (or the PB Ghost option in Dragon Escape stats), then rerun the same map. Confirm a full player model using the owner's skin appears semi-transparent, follows the recorded PB movement and pose continuously, is visible only to its owner, has no collision/hitbox, and disappears when the PB time is reached.
3. Confirm the PB delta remains in the action bar even when the ghost is off, updates through the final three-block finish radius, and changes sign when the current run moves ahead of or behind the PB.
4. Run with multiple racers. The sidebar and player list should agree on ranking, show the leader's elapsed time, show approximate `~+` gaps for trailing racers, and mark eliminated racers `OUT`. The sidebar should restore each player's pre-race scoreboard after the match.
5. Change maps and confirm PBs and checkpoint splits stay isolated per archive. Restart the server and confirm saved PBs, splits, and ghost preference persist.
6. Repeat on a tight switchback map and with Warper to verify checkpoint progress remains monotonic without false shortcut eliminations; separately confirm Douglas's chase and Digger/Disruptor/Leap interactions do not corrupt split timing.

The estimated time gaps use course distance at a fixed display pace; they are presentation estimates, not authoritative race timing. Build/unit checks do not replace live acceptance on the isolated Paper 26.3 server.


#### Death Tag parity continuation

The modern Death Tag port now resolves environmental damage with the legacy Iron Skin exclusions, gives the Null and Archer runner kits their perkless unarmed melee hit, and applies default melee knockback where the source perks do not suppress it. Every Archer arrow now follows the source 300% knockback hit path, including the final arrow in the inventory. Chasers use native Skeleton / Wither Skeleton visual proxies while the real player remains the combat entity; melee and projectile hits on the proxy are forwarded to that player. This is a server-side visual approximation, not a packet-level player disguise. Living players on both sides receive the source-style nearest-opponent Tracking Compass with distance and height feedback.

The Death Tag sidebar shows live Runner and Chaser counts. Forced time-based Alpha promotions happen in place, and converted runners retain their death-location respawn; defeated Chasers respawn at a configured Chaser spawn. ## Speed Builders — isolated source-map WIP

Speed Builders Arcade loads the original Reborn Shroomland Lake archive, the authored 7×7×7 template library and per-template ground states, the eight source player plots and central judging plot. Gameplay uses source-style view/build timers, template time modifiers, block-state scoring, mob markers/spawn-egg reconstruction, plot-bounded building and instant demolition refunds. The Arcade variant is **non-elimination**: every connected builder receives a normalized accuracy score out of 100 each round, remains in play regardless of score, and contributes to a configurable five-round cumulative total (default `speed-builders.match-rounds: 5`). Highest cumulative accuracy wins. Cumulative build time is a tie-break only for players who perfected every build; only their times appear on the final scoreboard. If no tied top scorer perfected every build, the accuracy tie remains shared. Gwen is a non-attacking Elder Guardian judge who looks between build plots; there is no attack beam, plot explosion, Guardian conversion or player elimination. All builders briefly enter spectator mode together to review the central model, then return to their own plots for the next round. Local stats and durable player-state snapshots are retained. The module remains outside the normal `READY_FOR_TEST` rotation pending Paper build and live multiplayer/map acceptance; see [SPEED-BUILDERS-TEST-PLAN.md](SPEED-BUILDERS-TEST-PLAN.md). Test commands: `/arcadeport play speed-builders`, `/arcadeport join speed-builders`, `/arcadeport start speed-builders`, and `/stats speed-builders`.
